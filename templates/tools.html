{# --- Season Management Tools --- #}

Season Management

Divide All Salaries by 2

This will divide every player's salary by 2 in the database. This reduces the salary costs for all teams.

Process Salary Bills (All Users)

Directly deduct salary bills from all users' budgets and create blog posts about the payments.

🏆 End of Season Process

Complete end of season processing that includes salary bills, player aging, contract updates, and salary increases.

📝 CPU Contract Renewals

Process contract renewals for CPU teams. Players with expired contracts (contract_years_remaining = 0) will be offered new contracts. 90% success rate.

🧹 Market Management

Clear all active market bazaar listings and offers. This will expire all current transfer listings and CPU offers.

💰 Recalculate Player Market Values

Recalculate all player market values based on their current salaries and age using the advanced game mechanics algorithm. Salaries remain unchanged.

🆓 Recalculate Free Agent Salary Demands

Recalculate salary demands for all free agents (No Club players) using the advanced game mechanics algorithm. This updates their salary requirements based on current skills and age. Draftees are excluded from this recalculation.

👴 Retire Player & Generate Regen

Manually retire a specific player and generate a new regen to replace them. This uses the same regen generation system as the end-of-season process.

Usage:

  • Step 1: Use "Divide All Salaries by 2" to reduce salary costs
  • Step 2: Use "Process Salary Bills" to directly deduct from all users' budgets
  • Result: Users' budgets are updated and blog posts are created automatically
